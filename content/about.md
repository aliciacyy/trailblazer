## Hello 👋
For the longest time in my life, I never liked running. I could barely run 1km without feeling like I had to stop.

I finally got into running in the second half of 2024, thanks to W. To be exact, the first time was on Aug 11th, 2024 when we ran 3km. Twice.

Since there, I was inspired to keep running as a way to keep myself in shape. Later on, this also served as a motivation for me to start this side project to try using Hugo.

## Trackers ⌚

I was using a Fitbit Sense 2, together with the Strava app, to track my runs until I got a Garmin Forerunner 165. Though I do continue to use the Strava app together with Garmin.

Map routes from August - November 2024 are from Strava due to some issue with the Fitbit app not showing my running routes for some reason...

Map routes from December 2024 onwards are from the Garmin Forerunner 165.
